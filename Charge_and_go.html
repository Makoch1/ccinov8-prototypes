Prototype Testing Guide

This interactive prototype simulates the user experience of finding EV charging stations with integrated, contextually relevant sponsored offers.

How to test:

  1. Interact with the map area on the left (click pins to simulate selection).
  2. Open the "View List" bottom sheet to see how stations are sorted.
  3. Look for the SPONSORED badge on specific stations (e.g., Station 2, Station 5).
  4. Click a station to view its details. If it has a sponsored offer, it will be prominently displayed.
  5. Click "Claim Offer" to simulate conversion and trigger the feedback modal.
  6. Watch the metrics update on this dashboard in real-time as you interact.

Goal: Validate if users find sponsored retail offers (coffee, food, shopping) useful while waiting for their EV to charge, without feeling the map experience is degraded.

Ad Placement Testing Goals

1. Sponsored Pin (Map)

Highlighted icon on the map.

Observe: Does the unique pin style draw clicks without frustrating users looking for regular chargers?

2. Station Detail Card

"While you charge" amenity offer.

Observe: Do users perceive this as a helpful nearby amenity or an intrusive ad blocking charger info?

3. Pre-Navigation Offer

Interstitial before routing starts.

Observe: Will users accept a slight detour for a relevant reward before they begin driving?
